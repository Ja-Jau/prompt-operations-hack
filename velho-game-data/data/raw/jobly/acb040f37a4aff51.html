Manager, Group Control – Lead Group Management-reporting and Planning Excellence

We are looking for a Manager, Group Control to take ownership of the Group’s management-reporting and annual planning processes. You will ensure timely, reliable and well-controlled reporting and planning, working closely with finance colleagues across businesses, countries and functions.

You will join at a time when we are actively bringing AI and data-driven ways of working into our finance processes. This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced finance professional who wants to make a visible impact at Group level and contribute to the development of reporting and planning.

This is a fixed-term position covering maternity leave until autumn 2027, with a preferred start as soon as possible. The position is based in Espoo, Finland.

Your responsibilities

  • Lead the Group management-reporting process, including the reporting calendar, management-reporting close, validation and quality controls.
  • Own and coordinate the annual planning process, including the planning calendar, instructions, control gates, Group-level validation and closure.
  • Manage the Group estimate submission process and Group-level validation, and govern finance master data, including cost centre and overhead structures, hierarchies and allocation principles.
  • Partner with Finance Development and relevant process owners to identify and define AI-enabled improvements to reporting and planning controls, validation and reconciliation, and support their implementation.
  • Collaborate across functions to define reporting requirements for Oracle HFM and planning requirements for Anaplan, and ensure reporting and planning requirements are reflected in AI-enabled forecasting and analytics developments.

Expectations for you

  • You hold an M.Sc. degree in Accounting, Finance, or another relevant field.
  • You have several years of experience in group reporting, consolidation, controlling, or FP&A within an international organization.
  • You have a strong understanding of management reporting, planning processes, and IFRS-based group reporting.
  • You can effectively lead processes and collaborate with stakeholders across organizational boundaries.
  • You have advanced English communication skills, both written and spoken.

Advantageous experience:

  • Oracle HFM, Anaplan, or other EPM platforms.
  • AI-enabled solutions for finance processes, reporting, or data validation.
  • Power BI or similar business intelligence tools.

To succeed in this role

You bring a structured and proactive approach to finance operations and enjoy taking ownership of critical processes. You are comfortable working with multiple stakeholders, balancing detail-oriented execution with continuous improvement. Your analytical mindset helps you identify opportunities to strengthen reporting quality, planning accuracy, and process efficiency through automation, AI, and data-driven ways of working.

We also expect you to align with Valmet’s Leadership Principles—practical behaviours that guide how we lead, collaborate, and succeed together:

  • Take accountability – Act with ownership, set clear expectations, and follow through
  • Lead with courage – Champion change, try bold ideas, and learn quickly
  • Build on trust – Listen to understand, be open, and create psychological safety
  • Collaborate for growth – Work across boundaries, value diverse perspectives, and focus on customer value

Why join our team?

You will join a collaborative Group Control and Finance Development team and take ownership of business-critical finance processes that support decision-making across the organization. This role offers broad visibility, close collaboration with finance leadership, and the opportunity to influence how AI and data-driven approaches are embedded into finance operations. You'll work in an international environment where continuous improvement and innovation are actively encouraged.
