Order Delivery Coordinator

  • Hae paikkaa

Are you interested in working with order and delivery process within a large global company? At our Sandvik’s Underground Drilling division, we are looking for two Order Delivery Coordinators to strengthen our Order Desk team in Tampere. Both positions are temporary until the end of December 2027. 

Your role

The roles cover a broad range of responsibilities within the end-to-end order and delivery process, from customer order management to outbound logistics and international deliveries. The balance between different responsibilities will vary based on your experience, strengths and the needs of the team.

Your key responsibilities

  • Receiving and processing customer orders for mining and tunnelling equipment and components, including validating the commercial and technical information of orders and ensuring accurate order handling throughout the process.

  • Planning and coordinating global transportation of equipment and components, evaluating transport alternatives and selecting suitable solutions based on customer, delivery and commercial requirements.

  • Preparing export documentation and invoices in compliance with prevailing laws and regulations.

  • Communicating with internal and external stakeholders, supporting our global sales organization and working in close co-operation with production planning, packing department and other supply chain functions.

Your background and what we find important

You have a suitable educational background in business, supply chain management, logistics or another relevant field, along with a good understanding of international trade and preferably some experience in order-to-delivery, order management and/or logistics processes.

Experience in customer order handling, delivery and export documentation, invoicing, customs processes, transportation or freight coordination is considered an advantage. You do not need to have experience in all of these areas, as the responsibilities can be balanced based on your existing expertise and strengths.

You communicate fluently in both Finnish and English, written and spoken, as both languages are used in your daily work.

To succeed in the role, you have good collaboration and organisational skills, a customer-oriented and proactive way of working. You are comfortable managing multiple tasks and changing priorities. You enjoy working as part of a team while also being able to work independently and make decisions within your area of responsibility

What is in it for you?

We offer you an interesting role in a multi-cultural, diverse environment with an excellent local team nearby always assisting and supporting your development. In addition, we offer good employee benefits, possibility to work partially remotely, and an inclusive corporate culture focused on sustainable development, with values such as Winning together, Curiosity, Responsibility and Customer focus. At Sandvik, we believe that diversity of experience, perspective, and background will lead to a better environment for our employees, our business, and, thereby, our customers.

About us

Sandvik is a global, high-tech engineering group providing solutions that enhance productivity, profitability, and sustainability for the manufacturing, mining and infrastructure industries. We are at the forefront of digitalization and focus on optimizing our customers’ processes. Our world-leading offering includes equipment, tools, services and digital solutions for machining, mining, rock excavation, and rock processing. In 2025, the group had approximately 42.000 employees, sales in more than 150 countries and revenues of about SEK 121 billion.

The Underground Drilling division within Business area Mining is an industry leading global provider of drilling equipment for underground mining and tunneling industry with focus on technology development and future innovative solutions to enable sustainable operations for our customers. Sandvik Mining and Construction Oy’s Tampere site is a global technology and manufacturing center for Underground Drilling equipment.
