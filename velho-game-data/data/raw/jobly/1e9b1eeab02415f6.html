WE ARE HIRING: SOURCING MANAGER – MECHANICAL CATEGORY

Are you an experienced procurement professional with a passion for global sourcing and supplier development? We are looking for a Sourcing Manager to join our Supply Chain team in Espoo, with responsibility for the mechanical category. In this role, youll report to the Head of Supply Chain and play a key part in developing sourcing strategies, negotiating with global suppliers, and driving continuous improvement across our operations.

The primary focus of this role is to drive strategic sourcing initiatives that strengthen supplier relationships, improve total cost of ownership, mitigate supply risks, and support business growth. In addition to leading category management and supplier negotiations, you will also contribute to operational procurement activities when needed, ensuring continuity of supply and supporting the wider purchasing team with tasks such as purchase order management, delivery expediting, and supplier communication. Success in this position requires both strategic thinking and a willingness to be hands-on when required.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

As our Sourcing Manager, you will:

* Develop and implement sourcing and category strategies for the mechanical category to optimize cost, quality, delivery performance, and supply continuity.

* Lead supplier identification, qualification, onboarding, and contract negotiations, including the introduction of new suppliers to support business growth, risk mitigation, and competitiveness.

* Ensure supplier capacity and capability are aligned with current and future business requirements, proactively managing supply risks and supporting production needs.

* Drive cost optimization, value engineering, and supplier development initiatives to deliver sustainable improvements in performance and total cost of ownership.

* Utilize SAP extensively in daily operations, maintaining supplier master data, tracking deliveries, monitoring supplier performance, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

* Improve procurement processes through digitalization and continuous improvement initiatives, leveraging SAP and other procurement tools.

* Collaborate closely with Production, Engineering, Quality, and Finance teams to ensure sourcing strategies, supplier capabilities, and capacity plans support business objectives.

* Build and maintain strong supplier relationships, fostering collaboration, continuous improvement, and long-term strategic partnerships.

* Actively support operational purchasing activities when required, including delivery expediting, escalations and supplier communication, to ensure uninterrupted supply and smooth supply chain operations.

WHAT WERE LOOKING FOR

This is a senior-level role for someone with solid hands-on experience. Were looking for someone with:

* A Bachelors or Masters degree in supply chain, Engineering, or related technical field

* At least 5 years of proven experience in strategic sourcing, category management, or procurement of mechanical components (mechanical category experience is required) within a demanding industry such as semiconductors, medical equipment, automation, or a comparable field

* Strong background in global sourcing and supplier negotiations, with a track record of delivering results independently

* Hands-on expertise with SAP (or equivalent ERP), with proven ability to use it as a daily working tool for procurement and supplier management

* Excellent negotiation, relationship management, and stakeholder collaboration skills

* A proactive, results-oriented mindset with real readiness to balance strategic work and hands-on operational tasks

* Willingness to travel occasionally

WHY JOIN US?

This role offers the opportunity to make a significant impact on our supply chain strategy and efficiency. You will be part of a dynamic and collaborative team, working at the intersection of strategy, operations, and innovation.

If you are ready to take the lead in shaping sourcing strategies, building strong supplier partnerships, and driving value across the supply chain, we would love to hear from you.
