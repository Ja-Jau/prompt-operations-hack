Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

  • Senior Advisor in the Site Mobility & Compliance team at Wärtsilä Energy based in Helsinki, Vaasa or Turku with hybrid work options.
  • You will manage assignee compliance including employee taxation, immigration and social security at project or country level and ensure adherence to regulations and Wärtsilä policies.
  • The role requires a relevant Master's degree, 5–6 years' experience in international personnel taxation, strong knowledge of posted worker and social security rules, and fluency in English and Finnish.
  • The position offers participation in Wärtsilä's global mobility transformation, collaboration with internal and external stakeholders on global projects, and opportunities to improve compliance processes.

We are always on the lookout for a talent with a growth mindset – are you the one?   

We are looking for a Senior Advisor into our Site Mobility & Compliance team at Wärtsilä Energy. The locations for this position are Helsinki, Vaasa or Turku. We offer a possibility to hybrid work. 

This would be an ideal position e.g. for someone with strong background in global mobility taxation looking for a 
move to an in-house role and interest in expanding one’s area of expertise into a more holistic mobility role. 
Site Mobility team handles assignee compliance for Engine Power Plants projects. We work closely with 
various internal and external stakeholders such as global mobility vendor, project taxation team, finance, local 
HR’s and group global mobility and tax teams. This is a new role which has been established due to business 
growth. Comprehensive induction to Wärtsilä and the role will be offered. 

Your role and responsibilities   

As a Senior Advisor your work will include managing employee taxation, immigration and social security related 
processes and questions together with our global mobility vendor. You will have project and / or country level 
responsibility of the overall compliance process. You will ensure that regulations and Wärtsilä policies (inc. 
working time) are followed and help problem solve ad hoc questions. 

You will be working independently as part of an experienced and friendly mobility team. We cooperate and find 
answers together and support is always available. In this role you will work together with project organization to 
support them from the project sales and planning phase to project execution. Depending on your interests, 
there are opportunities to take part in Wärtsilä’s development projects and continuously improve compliance 
processes in cooperation with other stakeholders. Our work is global and many of our projects are currently in 
the Americas region. 

Wärtsilä is currently in a global mobility transformation process, meaning you will have a chance to assist in 
change management in moving into a globally harmonised process and ways of working within our team. You 
will also be able to utilize modern mobility technologies in your daily work. 

Your work will focus on these responsibilities: 

  • Managing assignee compliance for our site assignees
  • Support in planning from mobility compliance point of view
  • Participate in continuous development 

To be able to succeed in the position, we expect you to have: 

  • Master’s degree in relevant field, for example tax or public law or economics
  • 5-6 years of experience in international personnel taxation for example in a professional services firm
  • Good understanding of posted worker and social security compliance
  • High level understanding and interest in immigration compliance
  • Understanding of EU GDPR in the context of mobility compliance work
  • High level understanding of project delivery related tax questions
  • Good communication skills
  • Proactive mindset, you take responsibility and action when needed
  • Good project management and prioritization skills
  • Fluency in English and Finnish.
