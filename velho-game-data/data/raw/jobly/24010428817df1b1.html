We are a global technology company developing innovative solutions for mining, tunnelling, and construction, improving project safety, environmental impact, and productivity. We offer premium equipment, all-round aftermarket services and high-quality chemicals and rock support as our core products to our customers. We operate globally in over 30 countries with more than 1,800 experts. At Normet, we commit to a culture founded in our Values - Caring, Committed & Courageous. We foster your talent, with us you shine.

We are looking for a   

 

Sourcing Specialist 

 

To lead sourcing activities for electrical components supporting EQ Iisalmi operations and global spare parts. The focus is primarily on standard electric components. The position is preferably based in Iisalmi. Other locations in Finland (Tampere or Espoo) may be considered. 

 

Main responsibilities and tasks:  

 

  • Source electric components for EQ Iisalmi operations and global spare parts needs 
  • Identify parts without an assigned supplier or trade agreement by monitoring Planned Purchase Order (PPO) for EQ Finland operations 
  • Select suppliers based on established supplier strategies and identify new suppliers when needed 
  • Negotiate supply agreements including prices, lead times, payment terms, warranties, and buffer stock agreements 
  • Manage annual price negotiations. Monitor cost competitiveness, supplier quality, and delivery performance. Define required corrective actions when needed 
  • Maintain vendor data in ERP systems within own responsibility area 
  • Work closely with engineering and advise the team in supplier and component selections 
  • Coordinate required actions following component end-of-life announcements, with engineering, production and spare parts teams to secure production and service continuity 
  • Define supply strategies 
  • Contribute to process and tool development in procurement area 

 

 

To shine in this position, we hope you have:   

 

  • M.Sc. or B.Sc. degree or equivalent professional experience. 
  • At least 2 years of experience in procurement and sourcing tasks in a relevant field. 
  • Analytical capabilities and systematic ways of working with a mix of attention to detail and strategic thinking 
  • Good negotiation and influencing skills 
  • Understanding of Normet supplier base and capabilities 
  • Excellent capability to work cross-functionally 
  • Good networking and relationship management skills 
  • Fluent written and spoken English  
  • Proficient in ERP, PDM and analytical tools (e.g. Excel, using AI) 
  • Ability to work under pressure 
  • Strong knowledge of parts and part types used by Normet
