Solution Manager

  • Hae paikkaa

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

  • Neste is hiring a full-time permanent Solution Manager for its Center of Excellence in Espoo, Tallinn, Riga, or Vilnius, reporting to the Head of CoE.
  • The role leads ERP and CRM implementation and delivery of Microsoft D365 F&O and CE solutions, designs standardized solutions, manages project execution, and later focuses on production support and continuous system development.
  • The role requires at least 5 years' relevant experience, a bachelor’s degree or higher, solid Microsoft D365 (CE and F&O) knowledge, strong cross‑functional collaboration skills, and fluency in English.
  • Applications must be submitted via Neste’s recruitment system by October 25, 2026, and the process may include interviews, an aptitude assessment, a health examination with a drug test, and possible security clearance.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.

Neste Marketing & Services, is one of the three Neste Corporation business units and responsible for the direct sales and marketing of a wide range of high-quality fuels including Neste MY Renewable DieselTM in its markets in Finland and all Baltic countries. We at the Marketing & Services business unit play an important role in connecting Neste's production with the end customers: consumers, transport service providers, customers in the aviation, shipping, industrial and agricultural sectors, municipalities, heating fuel customers, and fuel distributors. With our lower-emission and digital solutions we  respond to the changing customers’ and partners’ needs in its markets.

 

We are looking for you,


Solution Manager


to our Center of Excellence team in Espoo, Tallinn, Riga, or Vilnius. This is a full-time, permanent position.

You will play a key role in an ERP and CRM implementation project implementing Microsoft D365 F&O and CE solutions. Solution Manager role focuses on driving the successful delivery of complex projects by designing innovative solutions, managing project execution, and ensuring alignment with business objectives and stakeholder expectations. A Solution Manager is expected to understand relevant business processes and collaborate across the area, while actual process development responsibility lies with the Business Process Development team.

 

Along the ERP journey, we will also identify and build a roadmap for future system development. Following the implementation of ERP and CE, the role will strongly focus on production support and continuous system development. The CoE team works closely with both business and IT. In this role, you will report to the Head of CoE.

 

Reasons to join

We operate in an inspiring and evolving business environment as a global leader in renewable fuels. We contribute to Neste’s success by living our values every day: we care, we have courage, we cooperate.

We prioritize safety at all times and in every location. We strive to create a positive and impactful work environment.

Transformation brings new possibilities and fosters our courage and curiosity. As a global  company, we support personal development with versatile learning and career opportunities. Collaboration is key, and we believe in flexible, smart ways of working. We work at the office at least three days a week to ensure seamless teamwork and a sense of belonging.

In addition, our benefits for employees in Finland include, for example, a personnel fund as part of the overall remuneration, good occupational health care, a membership in a sickness or health insurance fund and leisure time accident insurance. We also support leisure time activities, e.g. by offering benefits for sports and culture (ePassi).

More about the job

 

Your role in our team 

 

  • Ensures that designed solutions are suitable for Neste M&S as a whole. Guides development towards global and standardized solutions across all operating countries.

  • Creates and participates in solution design together with key stakeholders.

  • Actively works on ideas to simplify and focus on the core solutions and applications that M&S business requires. Brings ideas for discontinuation when a specific solution or application does not match the previous targets or does not bring any additional business value. 

  • Plans, aligns and prioritizes roadmap items together with Business & IT. 

  • Provides solution advisory for business line organizations, projects and key users. 

  • Supports key users in Marketing & Services to define and maintain solution-specific documentation.

  • Collaborates closely with the CoE Process Development and Master Data teams.

 

The best combination to success in this role

 

  • At least 5 years of relevant work experience

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in a relevant field, such as Business or Technology/Engineering

  • Solid knowledge and experience of the Microsoft D365 ecosystem, preferably covering both CE and F&O

  • Excellent collaboration skills in a cross-functional and intercultural environment with both internal and external parties

  • Proactive and independent approach on tasks and assignments, willingness to drive change and achieve results

  • Willingness to learn new skills and competencies on a continuous basis

  • Ability to work independently and find solutions even in stressful situations

  • Consultative, analytical and development mindset

  • Systematic and solution-oriented way of working

  • Proficiency in English, both written and spoken

 

 

Let’s get you started

We look forward to receiving your CV and application as soon as possible, but no later than October 25th, 2026, as we review applications and conduct interviews on a rolling basis during the application period.


If you have any questions regarding the process or the position, please get in touch with Anna Hongell, Head of CoE, +358 40 338 0131.

To ensure security and confidentiality, please submit your application through our recruitment system, as we are unable to consider applications sent via email.

 

The recruitment process will include interviews and a potential aptitude assessment. The person selected for the position must pass a health examination including a drug test. Depending on the position’s requirements, a security clearance may be conducted with your consent.

 

Neste in brief

 

Neste (NESTE, Nasdaq Helsinki) is the world's leading producer of renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), with production on three continents. Neste’s strategy focuses on growth in renewable fuels. The company’s renewables production capacity is expected to reach 6.8 million tons annually in 2027. Neste also produces high-quality oil products at its Porvoo refinery in Finland. The company has a network of nearly 1,000 fuel stations with expanding service offering, such as EV charging, in Finland and in the Baltics. Neste is included in many international indices for its sustainability performance. In 2025, Neste's revenue stood at EUR 19.0 billion. Read more: neste.com
