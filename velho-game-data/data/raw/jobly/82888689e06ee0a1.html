Manager, Treasury Accounting

  • Hae paikkaa

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

  • The Manager, Treasury Accounting will join UPM Group Treasury in Helsinki and lead treasury accounting in accordance with IFRS and Finnish Accounting Standards.
  • The role includes taking overall responsibility for treasury accounting, contributing to group reporting, supporting treasury budgeting and back-office operations, and maintaining the treasury risk management system.
  • Candidates must have in-depth knowledge of IFRS and Finnish Accounting Standards, a basic understanding of financial instruments and derivatives, at least five years of relevant experience, and strong English and Finnish communication skills.
  • The position reports to the Director, Treasury Operations and applicants must apply via UPM's online system by October 25, 2026, with contact details provided for further inquiries.

We are looking for a Manager, Treasury Accounting to join our Treasury Operations team in UPM Group Treasury. The position is in Helsinki, Finland.

Greetings from your future manager

“The Treasury operations team is looking for an experienced accounting professional to join our team. We handle both external and internal transacting of financial transactions, from execution with banks to accounting and reporting of these transactions. This role offers a unique opportunity to combine your interest in treasury topics with your accounting skills. You will gain a holistic view of a multinational corporate treasury operations and have an important role in ensuring an efficient and robust treasury accounting process.” - Kim Järvinen, Director Treasury Operations

What you will do

  • Take overall responsibility of Treasury accounting in accordance with IFRS and Finnish Accounting Standards
  • Contribute to Group level IFRS and Finnish Accounting Standards reporting
  • Provide support for Treasury accounting, budgeting and Back-office activities
  • Maintain accounting setup in Treasury Risk Management system and participate in development of the system
  • Collaborate with the entire Treasury team, as well as Finance and Tax operations

 

Who you are

  • You have in-depth knowledge of IFRS and Finnish Accounting Standards
  • You have a basic understanding of financial instrument and derivatives
  • You have the ability and motivation to learn and maintain Treasury system
  • You have minimum of 5 years of relevant work experience
  • You have good written and verbal communication skills in English, Finnish and other language skills are considered as an asset

Additional information about the position

The position holder will report to Director, Treasury Operations.

UPM Treasury is a centralized function managing liquidity, foreign exchange (FX) and interest rate risk, funding, and cash management globally at UPM.
