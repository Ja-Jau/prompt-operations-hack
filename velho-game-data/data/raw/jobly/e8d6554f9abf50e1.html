Sourcing Analyst

Established in 1878, Olvi Group is currently producing over billion liters of beverages yearly in different product groups: beers, soft drinks, mineral and vitamin waters, long drinks, sports and energy drinks, ciders and juices. We are proud of our broad product portfolio including our own brands and completed by international production and distribution partnerships. Our goal is to be the most wanted multi-local beverage house. We know the local consumers and markets, and launch hundreds of novelties each year. 

Olvi Group employs over 2 500 professionals in nine different countries. Our head office is located in Iisalmi where it all began in 1878. We focus together on offering moments of enjoyment in consumers day to day life with our versatile high-quality products. 

We are looking for a Sourcing Analyst to join Olvi Group and provide visibility and data-driven insights to support our sourcing and procurement processes.

As a Sourcing Analyst, your responsibilities will include:

  • Collecting, consolidating and analysing procurement data from various sources.
  • Developing procurement reporting, data quality, KPI metrics, dashboards and analytical tools.
  • Producing supplier- and category-specific analyses to support decision-making.
  • Identifying savings and development opportunities and preparing forecasts to support procurement needs.

To succeed in this role, you should have a relevant university degree, and at least five years of relevant work experience. Previous experience working with procurement processes is considered an advantage.

We are looking for someone who is motivated to develop data-driven decision-making and build solutions where data, analytics and automation help reduce manual work, including through the use of AI.

The role requires strong analytical skills and the ability to process, combine and interpret large volumes of data and translate them into meaningful business insights. You should also have strong interpersonal and communication skills and be able to communicate effectively in English.

A precise and systematic approach to work, together with the ability to adapt quickly to changing situations, will help you succeed in this role.

We offer you an interesting and diverse role in an international and evolving listed company. We invest in the well-being of our people and provide a safe, inspiring, diverse and positive working environment. The position is permanent, and the starting date can be agreed upon. The location for this position might be either Helsinki or Iisalmi.

For further information about the position, please contact Suvi Lehtonen at +358 458 4045. Phone enquiries are welcome on November 5th, 2026 from 16:00 to 17:00 and on November 17th, 2026 from 17:00 to 18:00, or by email at [email protected].

Interested? Submit your application by November 15th, 2026!

Our values – local, positive, respectful and agile – are guiding our work and co-operation with each other and our stakeholders. Sustainability is part of our day-to-day operations through the value chain. On top of developing our own operations, we want to develop the beverage industry to be more sustainable by helping our partners, customers and consumers make sustainable choices. Our sustainability work is recognized internationally as the TIME magazine has included us twice (2024 and 2025) on the list of the world’s 500 most sustainable growth companies.
