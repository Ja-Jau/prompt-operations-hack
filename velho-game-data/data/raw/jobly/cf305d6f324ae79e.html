Director, IT Sourcing Category

  • Hae paikkaa

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

  • You will lead UPM's global IT sourcing category covering software, cloud, infrastructure, IT services and technology platforms to develop sourcing strategies and supplier relationships.
  • You will deliver strategic sourcing projects, negotiations and supplier selection while driving commercial value, cost optimisation and supplier performance management.
  • The role requires experience in sourcing or procurement with knowledge of IT services and cloud, strong cross‑functional collaboration skills and fluent English.
  • The position is based in Helsinki or Tampere, reports to the Vice President, Sourcing, and applications must be submitted online by October 30, 2026.

We are looking for an IT Sourcing Director to lead our IT sourcing team within Indirect Materials and Services Sourcing.

 

 

Greetings from your future manager

 

“This role offers an opportunity to make a real strategic impact. IT sourcing sits at the intersection of technology, business transformation, risk management and value creation. We are looking for a sourcing leader who combines commercial expertise with curiosity, sound judgment and a collaborative approach. You will build trusted partnerships with IT and suppliers while helping us continuously improve the way we work. If you are motivated by shaping how UPM manages critical technology suppliers and strengthening sourcing capabilities for the future, I would be very pleased to hear from you.” – Nina Kivioja, Vice President, Sourcing, Indirect Materials and Services

What you will do

 

In this role, you will help shape how UPM sources, manages and develops critical IT supplier relationships. You will work closely with UPM IT, Businesses, Legal, Finance, Security and supplier partners to create value, manage risk and support UPM’s future business needs.

Our team’s ambition is to excel in spend management, secure competitive commercial terms, create measurable value and manage supplier and contractual risks through strategic sourcing. We also lead, manage and continuously improve end-to-end sourcing processes in close cooperation with UPM stakeholders and suppliers.

As IT Sourcing Director, you will lead a globally relevant sourcing area covering technology, IT services, software, cloud, infrastructure, consulting and other IT-related spend. You will play an important role in strengthening strategic category management, supplier relationship management, commercial governance and future-ready IT sourcing capabilities.

In this role, you will:

  • Lead the development and implementation of IT category sourcing strategies, sourcing plans and supplier business plans aligned with UPM’s IT strategy, priorities and business needs
  • Lead, manage and deliver strategic IT sourcing projects, negotiations and supplier selection processes across software, cloud, IT services, infrastructure, cybersecurity, consulting and technology platforms
  • Drive commercial value through competitive sourcing, demand and spend transparency, total cost optimization, contract management and supplier performance management
  • Strengthen supplier relationship management for critical and strategic IT suppliers, including governance models, business plans, performance monitoring, escalation paths, risk mitigation and value delivery
  • Support effective risk management across IT sourcing, including contractual, cybersecurity, compliance, sustainability, continuity and supplier dependency risks
  • Build and maintain market and supplier insights, including technology trends, supplier landscape developments, AI and automation opportunities, and emerging commercial models
  • Collaborate with UPM IT, Sourcing & Procurement Operations, Legal, Finance, Security, business stakeholders and suppliers to support efficient end-to-end sourcing processes and compliant ways of working
  • Lead, support and develop your sourcing category team to achieve high performance, deliver business impact and build future-ready sourcing capabilities

Who you are

  • You have experience in sourcing, procurement, supplier management, commercial negotiations or a related area, ideally within an international environment
  • You bring knowledge of IT services, software, cloud technologies, technology platforms or supplier ecosystems, and you have an interest in emerging technologies, AI, automation and future sourcing approaches
  • You are confident in developing sourcing strategies, leading negotiations, managing supplier relationships and creating measurable business value
  • You build strong working relationships and collaborate effectively across functions, cultures and organizational levels
  • You can balance opportunities and risks, simplify complex topics and bring people together around shared objectives
  • You communicate clearly and effectively in English and are comfortable engaging with stakeholders across different levels of the organization
  • You lead with integrity, transparency and an Aiming Higher mindset, and you are motivated to identify opportunities to improve ways of working and deliver better outcomes

We are looking for talent and potential. If your experience does not match every qualification listed above, we still encourage you to apply if you believe you can contribute to the role and grow with us.

Sourcing

 

UPM Sourcing is responsible for maintaining a supplier base that can deliver cost-competitive, innovative, and sustainable material and service solutions to UPM businesses globally. The sourcing function consists of proactive, business-minded professionals who are experts on the supply markets. By applying sourcing expertise, business insight, and supply market knowledge, the Sourcing organization advances UPM businesses' profitability and growth.

Our proactive approach to risk assessment is a key part of our operations. We continuously work with over 25,000 material and service suppliers to ensure efficiency, transparency, and responsibility throughout the entire supply chain. We assess environmental, social, and economic risks both globally and at the supplier level. With selected suppliers, Sourcing is building strategic, long-term cooperation to optimize cost and quality without compromising on sustainability and innovation.
