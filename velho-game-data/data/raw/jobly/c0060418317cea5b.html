Procurement Specialist, Data Centers

  • Hae paikkaa

Eltel Networks Oy is a leading provider of critical infrastructure services in the Nordics. We enable the digitalization and electrification of society through reliable solutions for power, communication and renewable energy networks.

Do you want to help deliver the next generation of critical infrastructure? Eltel is now looking for a Procurement Specialist to join our growing Data Center team.

This is an exciting opportunity for an ambitious professional with 3+ years of experience to take ownership of high-impact procurement processes from the bid stage through to successful project execution. You can be based at any of Eltel's 50 offices across Finland. We work in a hybrid model.

Your key responsibilities

-
End-to-End Procurement Management: Manage the full procurement lifecycle, from developing sourcing strategies and RFx activities through supplier selection, negotiations, purchase orders, and final contract execution.

-
Tendering & Subcontracting: Prepare, issue, and manage clear, highly detailed RFQs for equipment suppliers and specialist installation subcontractors.

-
Commercial & Technical Evaluation: Review quotations, support technical and commercial comparisons, and drive supplier selection.

-
Material Take-Offs: Assist estimators with material and equipment take-offs, including accurate cable quantity estimations.

-
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Engineering, Project Management, Quality, Sales, Logistics, and Legal teams to deliver effective procurement solutions and optimize supply chain efficiency.

-
Supplier Management: Maintain clear procurement records, tender registers, and seamless, continuous communication with suppliers and subcontractors.

What We Are Looking For

We are looking for a technically strong, energetic, and solution-oriented procurement professional who loves velocity, excels in dynamic, fast-moving projects, and wants to grow within a leading infrastructure company.

We expect you to have:

-
3+ years of experience in a procurement, sourcing, or purchasing role.

-
Background in building systems (talotekniikka / MEP), electrical installation, power systems, or complex industrial construction projects.

-
Proven track record in subcontractor tendering and managing competitive bidding processes with specialist subcontractors.

-
Solid understanding of data center construction, electrical installation methods, and general construction sequencing.

-
Professional communication skills with the ability to work effectively in English.

Desirable Skills (considered an advantage)

-
CAD drawings: Ability to read and work with CAD drawings.

-
Revit: Experience with Revit.

-
Bluebeam Revu: Proficiency in Bluebeam Revu or similar tools.

-
Industry Experience: Previous experience in data centers, building system projects, industrial facilities, or large-scale electrical construction projects.
