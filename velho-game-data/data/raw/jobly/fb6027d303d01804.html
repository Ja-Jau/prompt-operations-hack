Service Coordinator/Engineer

  • Hae paikkaa

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

  • You will coordinate and execute global marine automation service jobs and support customers as a Service Coordinator/Engineer.
  • You will manage quotations, orders and work orders in the ERP system and follow up open warranty cases.
  • The role requires a technical background or equivalent experience (preferably in marine automation), ERP/CRM familiarity, strong organisation and fluent English.
  • The position involves occasional travel, acting as substitute for the Service Manager with participation in a 24/7 emergency support line, and includes benefits and flexible working.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.

Are you a service-minded engineer who strive to find custom made solutions to help customers? We have a new opportunity for you!        

Valmet is known for its reliable marine automation. We have been intensively present onboard all types of ships where our solutions play an integral role. Our target is to make the vessels greener, more efficient and more profitable, thanks to the use of advanced automation and scrubber solutions. We are now looking for a Service Coordinator/Engineer within Marine Automation.

Your Main Responsibilities

As a Service Coordinator/Engineer your key duties lay within:

  • Support and service our Marine customers.
  • Planning/coordinating/administrating service jobs globally by using our extensive service network.
  • Follow-up on open warranty cases.
  • Maintaining and updating the quotation, ensuring traceability and accuracy.
  • Managing orders from quotation to invoicing, creating work orders in the ERP system, and coordinating timelines with involved parties.
  • Planning and conduct/ execute service on demand, upgrades. Supporting the execution team.
  • To be substitute for Service Manager and be part of 24/7 emergency support line.

What do we expect from you?

We’re seeking a dynamic and detail-oriented professional who thrives in a fast-paced, multi-layered global environment. The ideal candidate will have: 

  • A technical background or equivalent experience in customer service or project coordination, preferably within the marine automation industry. 
  • Experience with ERP systems (e.g., Infor LN) and CRM systems. 
  • Strong organizational and problem-solving skills. 
  • Excellent communication skills in English (written and spoken). 
  • An accurate, structured approach to work with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously. 
  • A proactive, can-do attitude and eagerness to learn. 

Experience of working on ships with electrical or data programming is preferred.

We also believe that you have an engineering background, with PLC and/or automation programming. Knowledge of marine ships engine systems is considered as an asset.

This position requires some traveling at times to customer, ships or Marine floating devices. You need to be able to have English as a working language, any other additional languages are considered an asset. It is also beneficial if you are experienced in working abroad. 

We offer

In addition to benefitting from a stimulating work environment based on continuous improvement and teamwork, our employees have access to a full range of benefits: group insurance coverage, professional development support and a pension plan, to name a few. We also offer flexible working arrangements.

Additional information

For more information about this position, you are welcome to contact Global Service Manager, Bartosz Uzieblo, [email protected]. Please note that we do not accept applications via e-mail.

Apply as soon as possible, but latest October 26, 2026.
