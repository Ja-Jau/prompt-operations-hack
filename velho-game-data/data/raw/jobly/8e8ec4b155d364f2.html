Senior Project Planner

  • Hae paikkaa

Tiivistelmä työpaikkailmoituksesta:

  • You will work as Senior Project Planner in Metso's MINPS Estimation and Scheduling team in Espoo with hybrid working options, planning medium-to-large capital expenditure projects from concept to commissioning.
  • You will develop and maintain Level 1–4 schedules, perform quantity and resource loading, update schedules weekly and monthly, and analyze deviations to recommend corrective actions.
  • You must have an engineering degree and strong experience in large EPC projects with proficiency in Primavera P6, Microsoft Project and advanced Excel.
  • Metso offers competitive compensation and performance-based incentives along with wellbeing programs, hybrid flexibility, growth opportunities and an inclusive culture.


Tämä tiivistelmä on luotu tekoälyn avulla.

Our world depends on rocks, minerals, metals and other raw materials – the building blocks of modern society. As an industry leader, Metso helps transform these essential materials to enable sustainable modern life.

Our solutions and services support everything from renewable energy and electrification to the infrastructure and digital economy shaping our future. Together, we help our customers meet growing demand while driving the industry toward a more sustainable use of the earth’s natural resources.

Join nearly 18,000 Metsonites worldwide, each bringing unique expertise and experience to positive change. At Metso, you’re supported by a growth culture built on safety, wellbeing, inclusion and collaboration.

This is your opportunity to make an impact and rise above the possible.

Job posting end date: 11/08/2026

We are looking for a Senior Project Planner to join our MINPS Estimation and Scheduling team in the Minerals business area. In this role, you will plan and coordinate engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning activities for medium to large capital expenditure projects, helping ensure work is delivered efficiently, on time and to a high-quality standard.

You will report to the Head of Project Planning, and the role location is Espoo, with a hybrid working model available.

Team you will belong to

You will join the MINPS Estimation and Scheduling team in Espoo. The team focuses on project planning and scheduling, supporting the development and delivery of large-scale international mining, metals and industrial capital expenditure projects.

The team works with project participants, third parties and customers to develop reliable schedules and support effective project execution.

What you'll do

•    Develop and maintain Level 1, 2, 3 and 4 project schedules from concept through commissioning for medium to large capital expenditure projects.
•    Apply project planning procedures, standards and practices, gathering input from project disciplines, functions, third parties and customers to establish baseline schedules.
•    Perform quantity and resource loading as required, ensuring schedules align with project scope, constraints and contractual commitments.
•    Update schedules weekly and monthly, prepare project reports and analyze deviations, bottlenecks and delays to recommend corrective actions.
•    Support change notifications by assessing schedule impacts and contributing to tracking and re-baselining activities.
•    Participate in project meetings and help develop and improve planning procedures, standards and systems.

What you'll bring

•    Engineering degree from a university of applied sciences or equivalent.
•    Strong experience in large capital expenditure projects, with a good understanding of workflows and processes in industrial Engineering, Procurement and Construction projects.
•    Proficiency in project planning techniques and tools, including Primavera P6 and Microsoft Project, as well as advanced Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel.
•    Basic understanding of contract terms and conditions in plant delivery projects.
•    Ability to work proactively and independently while collaborating effectively as part of a wider team.
•    Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, attention to detail and an understanding of customer needs.
•    Fluent written and spoken English, combined with strong teamwork skills and learning agility.

What's in it for you

At Metso, you will combine meaningful work with opportunities to learn, grow, and make an impact in a supportive and inclusive environment. You'll be part of a global team where collaboration, wellbeing, and continuous learning are part of everyday work.

We support your growth and wellbeing by offering:

•    Purpose-led work: Contribute to enabling sustainable modern life and transforming the industry.
•    Wellbeing: Access to wellbeing programs, occupational healthcare, and supportive services.
•    Rewards: Competitive compensation and performance-based incentives.
•    Flexibility: Hybrid working options to support balance and productivity.
•    Growth: Learning pathways, mentoring, internal mobility, and global collaboration opportunities.
•    Culture: A caring, collaborative, and inclusive workplace committed to helping you thrive.
