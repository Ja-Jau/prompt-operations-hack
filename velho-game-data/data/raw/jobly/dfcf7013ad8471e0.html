Senior Trader

  • Hae paikkaa

Do you want to join us in building a cleaner tomorrow? Gasum is a Nordic energy expert. Our purpose is to help our customers navigate the energy transition by offering cleaner energy and superior energy management services.

We are looking for a Senior Trader based in our Espoo or Risavika office. This position is permanent.

Our purpose is to secure access to cleaner energy for our customers. We help them navigate the energy transition towards continuously reducing emissions. Our areas of expertise are biogas, liquefied biogas (LBG), natural gas, liquefied natural gas (LNG) and electricity markets. We help our customers in navigating the energy market also through managing the risks and benefits of a changing market.

At Gasum, LNG & Natural Gas team plays a pivotal role in the sustenance and growth of our business. Our team of eight professionals is committed to/focused/responsible for managing our LNG procurement and financial positions and the Natural Gas market offering in Finland and the Baltics.

Main responsibilities in the role are:

- Proactively manages an assigned book of products by pricing and trading towards customers, counterparts and trading venues - Sourcing of LNG in the small scale segment - Proactively support the end-to-end gas value chain and profitability - Responsibilities for own counterparties and development of relations - Back-up and co-operation with colleagues in the LNG and Natural Gas team

What we expect from you:

- Minimum 10 years of experience in trading in energy markets - Deep understanding of risk management and hedging - High level of numeracy and strong analytical skills, combined with a hands-on approach risk management and optimization practices - Strong written and verbal communication in English - Ability to communicate complex trading challenges in understandable manner to expert peers and senior management - Demonstrated history of reaching set P&L goals and KPIs - Open minded, dynamic, flexible, decisive, strong drive and desire to succeed, and result oriented approach towards work

What we offer you:

- Interesting tasks in a Nordic work environment and a front row seat into the renewable energy transition
- A positive and encouraging work community that drives change together
- Development opportunities in our sustainable, innovative and inclusive company
- A forward looking and modern workplace and an encouraging team to support you
- Flexibility in terms of working remotely or at the office in accordance with job requirements
- Various employment benefits and events, please read more here: Gasum as an employer | Gasum

At Gasum, we live by our values Respect, Sustainability and Positive energy. Our ethical code covers looking after people and the environment alike. Our purpose is to help our customers get access to cleaner energy and energy market services.

We look forward to your application! Please apply latest by 1.11.2026, enclosing your CV and cover letter. For inquiries about the vacancy, please contact Head of Natural Gas & LNG, Lauri Riihimäki between 9:00 and 10:30 on the 29th of October on +358409220113.

Gasum values equality and diversity within the work community. We welcome applicants from different backgrounds, such as people of different ages, genders, and those belonging to different language, cultural, or minority groups. Learn more about our company.

Gasum is a Nordic gas sector and energy market expert. Our areas of expertise are biogas, liquefied biogas (LBG), natural gas, liquefied natural gas (LNG) and electricity markets. We own and operate biogas plants and an extensive network of gas filling stations in the Nordic countries. Our customers include industry and businesses that use a lot of energy as well as maritime and land transport providers.
